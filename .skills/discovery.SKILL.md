# Discovery

Use for raw stakeholder material or a meeting debrief. Read `reference.INTERROGATION.md` and `reference.PROJECT.md`. In prep mode, produce a prioritized question list outside the repository. In debrief mode, append—not rewrite—a dated entry to `docs/discovery.md` with current process, pain points in stakeholder words, requested outcomes, observed workarounds, vocabulary, and unanswered questions.

Do not decide scope, prescribe technology, or translate business words into technical ones. Hand unresolved questions to `/plan`.
